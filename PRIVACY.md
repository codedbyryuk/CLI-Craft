# Privacy Policy

**CLI-Craft** does not intentionally collect or sell personal information.

## Data Storage

CLI-Craft may store launcher-related data locally on the user's computer, such as usernames, settings, Minecraft versions, and downloaded files.

This information is not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

CLI-Craft may communicate with third-party services such as Mojang/Microsoft services and Modrinth to provide Minecraft installation, authentication, version, and modpack functionality.

Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## Data Collection

CLI-Craft does not include intentional advertising, analytics, or tracking operated by the developer.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the launcher gains features that change how user data is handled.

**Last updated:** 2026
